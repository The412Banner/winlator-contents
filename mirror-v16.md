Delivery copies of the **versionCode 16** layers for the in-app catalog — five layers, 8 files (arm64ec for all five; x86_64 for 11.0-2, GE 11.0-9 and DroidDeck-Proton 11.0-11.1). These are the same files as the build release in proton-wine — [`build-bionic-layers-20261030-v16`](https://github.com/The412Banner/proton-wine/releases/tag/build-bionic-layers-20261030-v16) — which carries the full notes and credits.

New in v16: **`sechost` and `advapi32` import `rpcrt4` directly again**, which stops a crash in Windows service-control calls on arm64ec. It closed GOG Galaxy games such as *DOOM (2016)* at start, and the same crash stopped the EA app's background service on v15. Our toolchain does not have the LLVM exit-thunk patch Valve now relies on, so Valve's earlier workaround (Billy Laws, *HACK: Avoid delay importing rpcrt4*) is back on the Proton 11 layers, and the Proton 10 layers get the same change.

They install into a new `<version>-arm64ec-16` slot beside the older ones, so nothing is overwritten and a container on an older build of the same layer is offered **Update layer → v16** with a revert snapshot.

DroidDeck-Proton 11.0-11.1 arm64ec is device-tested on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 (*DOOM (2016)* from GOG reaches its menus at 60 fps; it crashed at start on v15). The other layers are CI-built and **not device-tested yet**; x86_64 has not been device-tested. Proton 11.0-1 does not have the bug and stays in [v15](https://github.com/The412Banner/winlator-contents/releases/tag/bionic-layers-20261023-v15); GE 11.0-3 / 5 / 6 / 7 / 7.1, both CachyOS builds and DroidDeck-Proton 11.0-9.1 stay in [v14](https://github.com/The412Banner/winlator-contents/releases/tag/bionic-layers-20261007-v14).
